# Future Roadmap — Beyond the Hackathon

## Prospective Study Requirements

1. Multi-centre validation across regions
2. Diverse camera types and populations
3. Blinded comparison with ophthalmologist grading
4. Sample size calculation for targets

## Technical Improvements

### Near-term (6 months)
- Train on larger dataset (EyePACS full)
- Improve MA detection with attention mechanisms
- Real-time video fundus analysis

### Medium-term (1 year)
- Multi-modal fusion (fundus + OCT + clinical history)
- Federated learning across PHCs
- Active learning for continuous improvement

### Long-term (2+ years)
- Population-level screening programme integration
- Longitudinal tracking (progression prediction)
- Automated treatment recommendation

## Impact Measurement

- Preventable blindness cases avoided
- Time from screening to treatment
- Cost per DALY averted
- Coverage rate in target population
