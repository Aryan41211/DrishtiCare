## 1. Methods

When two or more reviewers annotate the same examples, the following statistics will be calculated:

| Statistic | Use Case | Interpretation |
|-----------|----------|----------------|
| Exact agreement | Any scale | Percentage of identical judgments |
| Weighted Cohen's kappa | Two reviewers, ordinal scale (0-3) | <0.20 poor, 0.21-0.40 fair, 0.41-0.60 moderate, 0.61-0.80 substantial, 0.81-1.00 almost perfect |
| Krippendorff's alpha | Multiple reviewers, any scale | Same interpretation bands as kappa |

**Target:** Cohen's kappa ≥ 0.60 (substantial agreement) for pilot

## 3. Disagreement Handling

When reviewers disagree:
1. Both original judgments are **preserved** (never overwritten)
2. Adjudicator reads both judgments and explanations
3. Adjudicator selects final grade or requests clarification
4. Adjudicated grade recorded separately
5. Disagreement category and reason documented

**Do not:**
- Automatically discard low-agreement examples
- Hide disagreements
- Change guidelines mid-pilot without versioning
