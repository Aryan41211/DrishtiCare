# SIH 2026 Logistics

## Timeline

| Date | Event |
|------|-------|
| July 31, 2026 | SPOC registration closed |
| August 21, 2026 | SIH 2026 officially launched |
| September 2026 | Internal hackathons (varies by college) |
| After internal round | SPOC submits PPT + video for national screening |
| December 2026 | Grand Finale — 36-hour non-stop hackathon at nodal center |

## Team Rules

- Team size: **6 members** (typically)
- **At least one female member mandatory**
- 1-2 mentors allowed
- Must be able to explain, modify, and defend every part of the solution

## Submission Stages

1. **Internal/Institute Round** — PPT + video demo
2. **National Screening** — SPOC submits on behalf of team
3. **Grand Finale** — 36-hour live hackathon at nodal center

## Grand Finale Format

- 36 hours non-stop
- Working prototype required
- Live demo to judges
- Judges may feed surprise inputs
- "Demo live, not hardcoded" is explicitly in SIH 2026 guidance

## What Judges Look For

Based on published rubrics:
- Problem Understanding & Impact: ~25%
- Innovation & Technical Excellence: ~30%
- Feasibility: ~20%
- Impact & Scalability: ~25%

## Critical Implication

**The finale is a demo, not a build sprint.** Arrive with a working system. Spend the 36 hours on polish, edge cases, and presentation.

## AI Tool Policy

- No blanket ban on AI-tool use during development
- Teams must be able to explain, modify, and defend every part
- Judges can and will probe whether you wrote the code
